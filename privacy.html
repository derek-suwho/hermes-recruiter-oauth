Privacy Policy for Hermes Recruiter

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Hermes Recruiter is a private personal automation used only by Derek Su to manage his own recruiting activity. It is not offered to the public.

Google data accessed

  • Gmail: read-only access to recruiting-related messages, including sender, subject, date, labels, snippets, and message content when needed to identify deadlines or required actions.
  • Google Sheets: access to read and edit recruiting trackers explicitly authorized by Derek.

How data is used

Google data is used solely to identify application confirmations, assessments, deadlines, interviews, offers, rejections, and other recruiting updates, and to maintain Derek's recruiting trackers.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and limited recruiting metadata are stored in access-controlled files on Derek's private server. Data is retained only for personal recruiting operations and may be deleted at Derek's request. Email content is not published.

Sharing and advertising

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. The application does not transfer Google user data to external AI services for advertising or model training.

Permissions and user control

Gmail authorization is read-only and cannot send, delete, move, label, or mark email as read. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Local credentials and retained metadata can also be deleted.

Security

Credentials are stored with restricted filesystem permissions. Access is limited to Derek's private Hermes environment. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security, but access is minimized through separate least-privilege OAuth tokens.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be submitted through https://github.com/derek-suwho.
