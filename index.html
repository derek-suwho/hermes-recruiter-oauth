Hermes Recruiter

Hermes Recruiter is a private, personal recruiting assistant operated for Derek Su. It monitors Derek's own recruiting inboxes for application updates and online assessments, and maintains recruiting trackers that Derek controls.

The application is not offered as a public service and is used only by accounts explicitly authorized by Derek.

Permissions

  • Gmail access is read-only. The application cannot send, delete, move, label, or mark email as read.
  • Google Sheets access is limited to reading and editing recruiting spreadsheets authorized by Derek.
